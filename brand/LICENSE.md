## What it covers

- In `brand/`: the logo, symbol, wordmark and lockups (`logo/`), the icons,
  favicons, store and social graphics (`assets/`), the preview renders
  (`previews/`), the guidelines page (`guidelines.html`) and the design tokens
  (`tokens/tokens.json`, `tokens/tokens.css`, `tokens/contrast.json`).
- In `fastlane/metadata/android/`: the icon, the feature graphic and the phone
  screenshots under `en-US/images/`. The icon and feature graphic are copies of
  files in `brand/assets/`; the screenshots are the app's own screens rendered
  from test data.
- Copies of those files, and files made from them, elsewhere in the
  repository: the website's favicons and logo (copied into `website/` by
  `website/scripts/sync-brand.mjs`) and the app's Clairix launcher icon
  (`android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_*.xml`, generated by
  `brand/tools/build_logo.py`).

## What it does not cover

- The fonts in `brand/fonts/`, which keep their own licence, the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1 (`licenses/OFL-1.1-Archivo.txt`,
  `licenses/OFL-1.1-JetBrains-Mono.txt`), including where they appear in the
  logo and the screenshots.
- The code that builds these files (`brand/tools/`, `brand/tokens/build.mjs`),
  which is under the GNU GPL version 3 like the rest of the repository
  ([LICENSE](../LICENSE)).

Where these files are part of the app or the website, the GNU GPL version 3
covers that program as a whole; CC BY-SA 4.0 is one-way compatible with GPL
version 3, so the files can be conveyed with it.
