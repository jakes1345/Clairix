Clairix is a personal security app. It helps you detect hidden cameras, Bluetooth trackers, spyware, suspicious Wi-Fi networks and suspicious cellular activity, and it provides end-to-end encrypted messaging and calls between Clairix users. ## 6. Detection is best-effort

You acknowledge and agree that:

- No detection method is complete. Clairix cannot find every camera, tracker, piece of spyware, rogue network or fake cell tower. Cameras with no line of sight, analogue wireless cameras, cameras that only record locally, trackers that do not use Bluetooth, and spyware with system or root privileges are among the things it cannot detect.
- Results are indicators, not proof. A device detected by Clairix is not conclusive evidence of surveillance, and a clean result is not proof that none exists. Always verify a finding through more than one method before acting on it.
- Cell-tower and Wi-Fi analysis are heuristic. They score behaviour against a baseline and can produce both false alarms and misses.
- Evidence reports record what the app observed. We make no representation about their admissibility or weight in any proceeding.
- Clairix depends on your phone's hardware and on Android. Features may behave differently across devices and Android versions, and some are unavailable on older versions.
