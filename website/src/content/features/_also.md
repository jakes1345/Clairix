**Drive.** Offline turn-by-turn navigation on OpenStreetMap data, United States only for now (the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico), with privacy-preserving live traffic: anonymous speed samples per road segment, never your GPS track, and nothing for roughly the first kilometre (a distance that varies per trip) or the final approach of a trip. Routing works offline; the background map shows only areas you viewed while online. Optional engine data from a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, and with an RTL-SDR stick a channel scanner and the aircraft around you; the scanner records only when you turn recording on, and keeps recordings encrypted on the phone. The stick runs on the phone's battery: below 15 % with no charger, drive mode stops it and the adapter until the phone charges. Navigation takes a position every second during a route; below 15 % with no charger, its live traffic, speed reports and check for a faster route slow to every 5 minutes.

**NFC.** Read and identify NFC tags and cards, and flag ones that look like clones or emulators.

**Clean link.** Share a link to Clairix, or select it in any app and choose Clean link, and it comes back without what is there to follow whoever taps it: campaign tags (utm_ and the like), ad and mail click identifiers (fbclid, gclid, msclkid, mc_eid and others), and the share ids that YouTube, Spotify, Instagram, X, TikTok and others add. A link through Google's, Facebook's, Instagram's or YouTube's redirect page is unwrapped to the address it carries. It is done on the phone from Clairix's own list of rules, without opening the link or asking any server; then Copy, Open or Share. While Discreet mode is on, Clean link is not offered in other apps.

**Timeline and evidence reports.** Every finding is kept in a timeline on your phone, and the Timeline says how long before old entries are deleted. Export a human-readable report or a machine-readable JSON file for the period you pick, and share it with whoever you choose. It gives every time in UTC and in your phone's local time, the Clairix version, your phone's model, Android version and security patch, your retention and tracker settings, a summary of each device that travelled with you, device details and positions, the apps with spy-level access (who installed each and when, what it can reach, its signing certificate), the phone's integrity checks, unlocks during your sleep window, the trust store and the hidden-camera sweep, with any photo you kept going along as a picture. Each report ends with a SHA-256 of its text and a signature by a key Clairix keeps in your phone's Android Keystore, so whoever receives it can check it was not edited (see Support). Before it shares, Clairix warns you that the report holds your own location history.

**A tour of the app.** After setup, a tour walks through every place in the app and what it does, with a button to open each one. It is built from the app's own lists, so nothing in Clairix goes untoured, and it can be taken again from Settings.
