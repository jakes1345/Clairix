**Drive.** Offline turn-by-turn navigation on OpenStreetMap data, with privacy-preserving live traffic: anonymous speed samples per road segment, never your GPS track, and nothing for the first 500 metres or the final approach of a trip. Optional engine data from a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter.

**NFC.** Read and identify NFC tags and cards, and flag ones that look like clones or emulators.

**Timeline and evidence reports.** Every finding is kept in a timeline on your phone. Export a human-readable report or a machine-readable JSON file with UTC timestamps, device details and positions, and share it with whoever you choose.

**A tour of the app.** After setup, a tour walks through every place in the app and what it does, with a button to open each one. It is built from the app's own lists, so nothing in Clairix goes untoured, and it can be taken again from Settings.
