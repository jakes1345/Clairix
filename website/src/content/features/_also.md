**Drive.** Offline turn-by-turn navigation on OpenStreetMap data, United States only for now (the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico), with privacy-preserving live traffic: anonymous speed samples per road segment, never your GPS track, and nothing for roughly the first kilometre (a distance that varies per trip) or the final approach of a trip. Routing works offline; the background map shows only areas you viewed while online. Optional engine data from a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, and with an RTL-SDR stick a channel scanner and the aircraft around you; the scanner records only when you turn recording on, and keeps recordings encrypted on the phone. The stick runs on the phone's battery: below 15 % with no charger, drive mode stops it and the adapter until the phone charges. Navigation takes a position every second during a route; below 15 % with no charger, its live traffic, speed reports and check for a faster route slow to every 5 minutes.

**NFC.** Read and identify NFC tags and cards, and flag ones that look like clones or emulators.

**Timeline and evidence reports.** Every finding is kept in a timeline on your phone. Export a human-readable report or a machine-readable JSON file with UTC timestamps, device details and positions, and share it with whoever you choose.

**A tour of the app.** After setup, a tour walks through every place in the app and what it does, with a button to open each one. It is built from the app's own lists, so nothing in Clairix goes untoured, and it can be taken again from Settings.
